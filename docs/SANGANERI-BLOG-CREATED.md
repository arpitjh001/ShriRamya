- Distinctive characteristics
- Complete 6-step dyeing process
- Care instructions

### 2. Step-by-Step Dyeing Process

**Step 1: Fabric Preparation (Bleaching)**
- Washing, bleaching with goat dung & soda ash, sun drying

**Step 2: Block Carving**
- Wood selection, design transfer, carving, handles

**Step 3: Color Preparation**
- Natural dyes from madder roots, indigo, pomegranate, turmeric

**Step 4: The Printing Process**
- Outline printing (Rekh), fill printing (Datta), alignment

**Step 5: Fixing the Colors (Steaming)**
- Wrapping, steaming, color bonding

**Step 6: Washing and Finishing**
- Washing, sun drying, calendering

## Content Highlights

### Key Statistics
- **Origin:** 16th century Sanganer, Rajasthan
- **GI Tag:** 2009
- **Production Time:** 2-3 days per saree
- **Blocks per Design:** 3-5 different blocks
- **Natural Dyes:** Red (madder), Black (iron), Yellow (pomegranate)

### Cultural Significance
- Supports traditional artisan communities
- Eco-friendly natural dye process
- Each piece is unique with slight variations
- Double-sided printing technique
- Mughal garden inspired motifs
